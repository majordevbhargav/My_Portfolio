About

I build software that sits at the intersection of AI and practical, everyday use: resume intelligence, career matching, and campus networking tools that people actually rely on, not demos that end at a pitch deck.

My path here wasn't a straight line. Before engineering, I spent years preparing for the armed forces, clearing the NDA written examination five times and training for the Services Selection Board. That process is where the discipline, composure under pressure, and follow-through I now bring to engineering actually came from.

Today I work as an Associate Engineer and network engineer at Velocis Systems Pvt Ltd, supporting enterprise networking and IT infrastructure, while building Career Match, an AI-powered career platform, on the side. I'm currently studying for CCNA, Cisco ISE, and Cisco XDR to deepen my networking and security skills. Longer term, I want to pursue research in Japan through the MEXT Scholarship.
